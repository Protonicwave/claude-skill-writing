# Higgs mass fit, notes

Data: ATLAS Open Data, 13 TeV, 10 fb^-1, diphoton channel.
Selection: two photons with pT above 35 GeV and 25 GeV, |eta| < 2.37, crack region 1.37 < |eta| < 1.52 removed.
Fit: double-sided Crystal Ball signal plus fourth-order Bernstein background, fitted from 105 to 160 GeV.
Result: m_H = 125.2 +/- 0.9 GeV, statistical uncertainty only. Signal yield 512 +/- 98 events.
Systematics not done yet. Photon energy scale will be the biggest one.
Comparison paper: the ATLAS discovery paper, doi 10.1016/j.physletb.2012.08.020. I think they got about 126 GeV.
