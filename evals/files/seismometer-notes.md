Group design project: low-cost seismometer for school outreach.
Final design: geophone and 24-bit ADC on a Raspberry Pi. Parts cost £84 against a target of £100.
Optimised the amplifier gain: noise floor 0.4 µm/s RMS in the 1 to 10 Hz band, down from 1.1 µm/s.
Modelling of the enclosure's thermal behaviour showed a drift of 2 µm/s per degree; foam insulation fixed it.
Three-week field test in a school: recorded 3 distant earthquakes, all matching events in the national catalogue. Data analysed with ObsPy.
Live display for pupils, colour-coded by shaking strength.
Not done: calibration against a reference seismometer (could not borrow one).
Recommendation for next year's group: do the reference calibration first.
