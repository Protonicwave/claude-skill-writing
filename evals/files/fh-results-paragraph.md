The collector current showed five peaks, at accelerating voltages of 7.1, 12.0, 16.9, 21.8 and 26.7 V. A linear fit of peak position against peak number gave a slope of 4.90 ± 0.03 V, which we take as the energy an electron loses in each inelastic collision with a mercury atom, and an intercept of 2.2 V, which we attribute to the contact potential between cathode and grid and did not investigate further because of the time left in the session. This proves that the first excitation energy of mercury is 4.90 eV.
