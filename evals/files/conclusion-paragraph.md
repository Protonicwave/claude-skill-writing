The simulated data was compared with the measured spectra over the full energy range. Agreement was good below 2 MeV, where the residuals stayed within the statistical uncertainty of each bin. Above 2 MeV the simulation overestimated the count rate by about 8%, which we attribute to the detector response model rather than to the source. The calibration data was taken on a single day, so a drift in gain over longer periods cannot be ruled out. A second calibration run, a month apart, would test this.
