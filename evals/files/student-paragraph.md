In today's rapidly evolving landscape of computational physics, machine learning has emerged as a crucial tool. Furthermore, it is important to note that neural networks can potentially offer significant speed-ups - sometimes by a factor of 40 - compared to traditional Monte Carlo solvers. Moreover, our surrogate model delves into the intricate dynamics of the 2D Ising model, achieving a mean absolute error of 0.012 in the magnetisation per spin across 64 temperatures between 1.5 and 3.5. This showcases the transformative potential of deep learning for statistical physics.
