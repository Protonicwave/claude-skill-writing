Dissertation submitted 6 May.
Classified glitches in LIGO data with a CNN trained on time-frequency images (Q-transforms).
94.1% accuracy across 22 glitch classes, held-out test set of 6,000 images.
Hardest pair: Blip and Koi Fish. 11% of Blips were labelled Koi Fish.
Supervisor: Dr Sofia Marin.
Lesson: cleaning the labels took longer than training the model.
