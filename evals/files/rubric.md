# PHY3002 project report: marking criteria for the Methods section

The Methods section is marked out of 10.

- Reproducibility (4 marks): another student could repeat the work from the text alone. Every software package is named with its version. Random seeds and data splits are stated.
- Uncertainty (3 marks): the procedure behind every quoted uncertainty is described.
- Style (3 marks): continuous prose in the past tense and the first person plural ("we trained"). Bullet points, sub-headings and results in the Methods section lose marks. At most 300 words.
